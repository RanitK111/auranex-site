Common questions

Will this replace my front desk?

No. It catches the calls your team can't take, such as during jobs and treatments, after hours and during rushes. Many customers keep their staff and use Auranex as backup and overflow.

Does it sound robotic?

The voice is natural and conversational, and it is set up around your business, tone and services. We also help you add a short disclosure that callers are speaking with an AI, which many US states and good practice recommend.

Do I need new phone equipment?

No. Calls forward from your existing business number. No new hardware and no downtime.

What if it can't answer something?

It takes a message and notifies your team immediately, or transfers the call to a live person if you enable that.
