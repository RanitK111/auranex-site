The problem

Plumbing customers call in a panic, usually when you're under a sink or off-shift. If they get voicemail, they keep dialing until someone answers.

What Auranex does

  • Picks up instantly, day or night
  • Captures the issue, address, access details and urgency
  • Separates emergencies (leaks, backups, no water) from routine jobs
  • Books estimates and jobs, or notifies your on-call plumber
  • Answers questions on service areas, call-out fees and hours
  • Texts you a clean summary of every call

Example calls

Illustrative scenarios of how a call can be handled. Exact behaviour is set up with you.

“There's water coming through my ceiling.”

Tells the caller to shut off the main if they can, gathers details, flags it urgent and alerts your on-call plumber.

“How much to replace a water heater?”

Explains how you quote, then books an on-site estimate.

Plumbing questions

Will it quote prices?

Only what you approve, such as a call-out fee or 'free estimates'. For anything else it books an estimate instead of guessing.

Does it work with my existing number?

Yes. Calls forward to Auranex, and you keep your current business number.
