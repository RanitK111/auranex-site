1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) are small pieces of data stored in your browser. They help websites work, remember choices and measure usage.

2. What this site uses

  • Essential storage. We store a single value in your browser's local storage to remember that you have seen our cookie notice. It is not used to track you.
  • Calendly (only if you load it). The demo booking calendar on our Book a Demo page is loaded only after you click to show it. Calendly may then set its own cookies under its own policy.
  • Fonts. We load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser details.
  • Analytics. At the time of writing we do not run an analytics or advertising tool. If we add one, we will update this page first.

3. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage will not stop the website from working, but you may see the notice again on each visit.
