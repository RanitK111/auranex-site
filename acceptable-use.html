This policy applies to everyone using the Auranex Service. We may suspend or end accounts that break it.

1. You must not use Auranex to

  • Break any law or regulation, including laws on telemarketing, robocalls, call recording, text messaging, privacy, consumer protection and discrimination.
  • Make unsolicited or deceptive calls or texts, spam, or contact people on do-not-call lists without a lawful basis.
  • Impersonate a real person, or pretend the AI is human when asked directly or when the law requires disclosure.
  • Mislead callers about your prices, services, licences, qualifications or availability.
  • Harass, threaten or defraud anyone, or collect payment card numbers, government ID numbers or passwords over the AI voice channel.
  • Provide emergency, medical, legal or safety advice through the AI, or position it as a substitute for emergency services.
  • Attempt to disrupt, probe, reverse engineer or overload the Service or its providers.
  • Upload malware or content that infringes someone else's rights.

2. Your obligations as a customer

  • Use a disclosure greeting where the law requires it (for example, telling callers they are speaking with an AI and that calls may be recorded).
  • Obtain and record consent before sending marketing text messages (see SMS Terms) and honour STOP requests.
  • Keep the information you give the AI accurate and current.
